Share your work with your teacher

Your work is saved in this browser, so your teacher can’t see it in CodeHS. Share it one of these ways.

I’m using a:

1. Turn in a PDF on Google Classroom Best

  1. Click Make a PDF. A print window opens.
  2. Next to Destination, choose Save as PDF, then click Save. It goes to your Downloads folder.
  3. Open Google Classroom and click the assignment.
  4. Under Your work, click + Add or create, then File, and upload the PDF from Downloads.
  5. Click Turn in.
  1. Click Make a PDF. A print window opens.
  2. In Chrome: next to Destination, choose Save as PDF, then click Save. In Safari: click the PDF menu at the bottom left and choose Save as PDF. Save it to your Downloads folder.
  3. Open Google Classroom and click the assignment.
  4. Under Your work, click + Add or create, then File, and upload the PDF from Downloads.
  5. Click Turn in.

The PDF shows the checkpoint, your name, your page, your Reflect and User flow answers, and your code. At the User flow checkpoint it shows your arrows, and at the Tags checkpoint it shows your tags. Type your name and pick the checkpoint first.

If Make a PDF doesn’t work, take screenshots instead
  1. Click Full screen first so your whole page shows.
  2. Press Ctrl + Show windows (the key with boxes, above 6) to capture the screen. Or press Ctrl + Shift + Show windows and drag to capture one part.
  3. Scroll down and take more screenshots until your whole page is captured. Open Show my code and take one more.
  4. Your screenshots go to your Downloads folder. In Google Classroom, open the assignment, click + Add or create, then File, add your screenshots, and click Turn in.
  1. Click Full screen first so your whole page shows.
  2. Press ⌘ Command + Shift + 3 to capture the whole screen. Or press ⌘ Command + Shift + 4 and drag to capture one part.
  3. Scroll down and take more screenshots until your whole page is captured. Open Show my code and take one more.
  4. Your screenshots go to your Desktop. In Google Classroom, open the assignment, click + Add or create, then File, add your screenshots from the Desktop, and click Turn in.

Tip: to show your User flow arrows, switch to User flow and take a screenshot there too.

2. Turn in your index.html file on Google Classroom

It keeps your pictures, words, sizes, and code.

  1. Click Download my index.html file. It goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open Google Classroom and click the assignment.
  3. Under Your work, click + Add or create, then File, and upload index.html from Downloads.
  4. Click Turn in.
